# Sources: Swiss Poster

## Lemo-Opuscar, style No. 16 "Swiss" (Swiss Motion Graphics)

- **Repo:** https://github.com/lemomo-ai/lemo-opuscar, `styles/swiss-motion/` (`STYLE.md`, `DEMO.md`, `style.json`);
  film https://github.com/lemomo-ai/lemo-opuscar/releases/download/films/swiss-motion.mp4 .
- **Research notes:** `docs/research/styles/five-skills.md`, section "Swiss Motion Graphics". Nothing from the repo was
  downloaded for this style.
- **Licence:** MIT for code and docs; demo films and stills CC BY 4.0 (credit LemoLab + link). No code was copied.
  Archivo is SIL OFL.

### Taken from it
- Page `#EAE9E5`, paper `#FFFFFF`, ink `#111111`, signal `#E30613`, grid lines `#CFCEC9` / `#C9C8C2` / `#E2E1DC`,
  secondary bars `#CFCEC9`; alternate sets `#F2F0EA / #141414 / #FF5A00` and `#E9ECEF / #0B0B0B / #1F3FFF`; one
  signal colour per film.
- Archivo (variable, 400–800); the type roles (title 120 px Bold in two lines; lead-in 30 px Medium grey + statement
  76 px Bold; rule numerals ~980 px; notes ~19 px Medium under a 1 px rule; sign-off 36 px); −2.2% tracking at
  ≥ 90 px; lowercase headlines; emphasis by scale and weight; subtitles as part of the layout.
- Grid logic (12 columns, 96 px margins, 24 px gutters, 24 px baseline; the artifact's grid derived from content,
  e.g. 7 × 16), bars (column − 8 px, merged), flush left, technical notes, the coordinate readout, the 2 px knife.
- Motion: `cubic-bezier(.7,0,.2,1)` and linear only; note-value durations at 120 BPM; clip reveals from below the
  baseline in 1/16, leaving in 1/8; lines growing; the paper's vertical wipe; numerals climbing in 4 steps; the knife
  cut in 1/16 and collapse in 1/8; ripples nearest first; the exception moving linearly with a pause and growing;
  the camera vocabulary; transitions (wipes, knife cuts, camera, re-flow; no dissolves); rhythm; no finish; the
  banned list.

### Proposed (not in the source)
- The grey `#6f6e6a` for lead-ins and notes (the source says "Medium grey" without a hex).
- The vertical grid (6 × 136 px columns, 72 px margins, 24 px gutters) and every size for 1080×1920.
- The sample content: the signal circle as the sun, seven wavelength bands as bars, the N₂ molecule as two dots with
  bars radiating on the grid's directions, the staircase diagonal on the end poster, the wording of notes. The note
  "(700 ÷ 450)⁴ ≈ 5.9" is the Rayleigh ratio behind the sample's number (5.86).

### Changed for vertical
- The source spread is landscape (programme on columns 1–6, poster on 8–12). Here programme and poster stack, the
  poster spans 5–6 of 6 columns, and type is scaled up (statements 96 px, notes 24–26 px).
