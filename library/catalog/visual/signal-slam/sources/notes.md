### Taken from it
- Field `#ff4d12` brightened ~12% at the centre; hatch `#c21d0b` at 16%, 9 px pitch, noise-varied; glow `#ff8a3d`;
  type `#0a0a0b`; corner labels Plex Mono (13 px, spacing 3, ink 55–80%).
- Archivo 900 uppercase, one word at a time, fitted wide, each at its own width (AND 125, THIS 112.5, IS 125, WHERE
  87.5, IT 125, GETS 100; CRAZY. 125).
- Slam 1.25 → 1 over 0.16 s ease-out-expo with a hard cut; the final word's stretch (0.05 s stagger, widths 62 → 125
  over 0.3 s, scale 1.3 → 1 over 0.15 s, 6 px jitter, half-life 0.12 s); readout roll over 0.6 s ease-out-expo;
  shutter close (ease-in-quart); the incoming keycap rush; the shrinking ⏎ (560 px → 0 over 0.35 s, ease-in-cubic).
- Finish: bloom 0.4 (threshold 1.2), vignette 0.3, grain 0.06, zoom punch +2% per word (half-life 0.07 s), shake
  14 px on the last word, aberration spiking to 4.2 px.
- Plate number top-left, readout bottom-right (220 px bar, 40 px digits).

### Proposed (not in the source; the source is one ~2 s example)
- Stack, sequence (with chevrons) and band-split beats; inverted ink blocks; the line strip and word counter; the
  shutter held as a letterbox end card with the CTA; the bone accent.
- All px sizes for vertical (column 960, max cap height 820, labels 24 px, digits 48 px); per-width character widths;
  the glow half-life (0.25 s); shutter (0.32 s), invert and band (0.1–0.14 s) timings; the derived muted colour.
- The sample readouts (`WDTH`, `RATIO`) chosen so every number shown is true.

### Changed for vertical
- Words are fitted to 960 of 1080 px instead of ~1500 of 1920, so wide words are shorter on screen; the furniture is
  inside the safe zones and scaled up; a line strip carries the sentence that one-word frames can't.
