# Sources: Pictogram

## Lemo-Opuscar, style No. 21 "Pictogram" (Pictogram Motion)

- **Repo:** https://github.com/lemomo-ai/lemo-opuscar, `styles/pictogram-motion/` (`STYLE.md`, `DEMO.md`,
  `style.json`); film https://github.com/lemomo-ai/lemo-opuscar/releases/download/films/pictogram-motion.mp4 .
- **Research notes:** `docs/research/styles/five-skills.md`, section "Pictogram Motion". Nothing from the repo was
  downloaded for this style; the `demo/ref/` images were not looked at.
- **Licence:** MIT for code and docs; demo films and stills CC BY 4.0 (credit LemoLab + link). **Caution from the
  notes:** the demo imitates a real event's identity; its palette values and colour names, mark, slogan and grid
  pattern art belong to the organiser and are not reused. Only the grammar is taken. No code was copied. Inter Tight
  and DM Mono are SIL OFL.

### Taken from it
- The colour system (five hues + an ink neutral, five tones each, `t2` base; one hue per chapter; pattern spread 0.42
  under type, 0.95 on chapter cards; disc tones by hue; one cream, one ink; light hues take dark type).
- Type: Inter Tight 900 titles (−0.02 em, 150–330 px range, auto-fit), DM Mono 500 meta with 3 px tracking, mono tag
  chips, outline index numerals at 20%, HUD in the four corners and a progress bar of ticks.
- Layout: the 180 px square grid; card layouts A, M, B, C and the chapter card; text and figure never overlapping.
- The pattern recipe (34% discs, 16% ringed discs, density 0.5 motifs from a generic family, 90° turns, a pattern
  wider than the screen so rows can drift) and its motion (rows drifting 26 px/s, quarter-cell jumps on chapter cards).
- The figure (proportions, widths, round caps, draw order, far limbs mixed 42% toward the ground, faceless), the
  stage (a sun disc with one 9% white sweep), props.
- Motion: the beat grid at 150 BPM, pace ladder, exponential ease-outs, mask slide-ups (0–0.5 s, +0.08 s, +0.2 s),
  typed meta, the 54 px band assembly over 0.42 s, the chapter band, count-up in one bar, whips; grid transitions;
  the finale; flat finish; banned list.

### Proposed (not in the source)
- The palette: an original coastal set (sky, navy, teal, coral, sand) built in five tones each, and generic dark
  greys for the ink neutral; the coral tag.
- Every size for 1080×1920; the vertical layouts; the poses (looking up, arms open, standing, reaching); the disc in
  `t0` on the sky card (in `t1` it disappears into the ground); twelve of the source's fourteen motif families.
- The cards' wording; the facts are the sample's own (450 nm, 700 nm, 5.9×). No second language.

### Changed for vertical
- `CELL` stays 180 px but is now 1/6 of the width; layouts stack text over stage; the ghost title runs on two lines;
  meta and HUD are 24–26 px instead of 17–22 px.
