# Sources: Risograph

## Lemo-Opuscar, style No. 12 "Risograph" (Risograph Print)

- **Repo:** https://github.com/lemomo-ai/lemo-opuscar, `styles/risograph/` (`STYLE.md`, `DEMO.md`, `style.json`); film
  https://github.com/lemomo-ai/lemo-opuscar/releases/download/films/risograph.mp4 .
- **Research notes:** `docs/research/styles/five-skills.md`, section "Risograph Print". Nothing was downloaded; the
  source's print shader was not read.
- **Licence:** MIT for code and docs; demo films and stills CC BY 4.0 (credit LemoLab + link). No code copied: plates,
  screens and ink texture are new SVG code from the documented values. Bricolage Grotesque and Jost are SIL OFL. The
  ink names are colour references only; no brand marks.

### Taken from it
- Paper `#F6F1E6`; inks Riso Blue `#0078BF` (15°), Yellow `#FFE800` (0°), Fluorescent Pink `#FF48B0` (75°); the
  alternative sets (Teal `#00838A` + Fluorescent Orange `#FF7477`; Federal Blue `#3D5588` + Red `#FF665E` + Sunflower
  `#FFB511`); multiplied overprints; the palette rules (2–3 inks, tints and overprints only, one-plate version, skin as
  a pale tint, the full overprint saved for the climax).
- Bricolage Grotesque 800 titles in capitals, the double-hit (10 px); Jost 500 captions, 0.5 px tracking, blue on a
  paper-white knockout strip with a 6 px radius.
- Stepped bands 20 / 38 / 62 / 100%; dots anchored to the paper; knockout halo 4–5 px; registration crosshairs on the
  end card.
- Motion: fixed registration per shot, the kick, the drift, grain boiling about a third, roller sweeps; overprint
  growths, roller sweeps, match cuts, hard cuts; banned list.

### Proposed (not in the source)
- Screen period 10 px; the 26% band; halo 4.5 px; kick 11 px / 0.07 s half-life / 7 Hz; roller 0.45 s with a 26 px
  band; grain 2.2 px; pinhole threshold; all sizes for 1080×1920; the three-disc Venn for "every colour"; wording.

### Changed for vertical
- Wide tracking shots with a small figure in a big sky become a centred dome of stepped sky bands with the figure's
  head against the sun; the title prints above and the key word below; captions sized for a phone.
