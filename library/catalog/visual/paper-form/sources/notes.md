### Taken from it
- Paper = bone × 0.965 in linear light (≈ `#eae5dc`, computed in the research notes), ink, graphite, signal, blood,
  bone; the band at 94% ink.
- Header: Archivo width 112.5 / 900; mono section labels (600, spaced); typed answers (Plex Mono 400 with ±0.8 px
  jitter, hot signal → ink over 0.35 s); box names (500), notes, fine print at 75%, options (500, spaced).
- Box outlines 1.6 / 2.4 px, "1.1" numbers, arrows 2.2 px with 14 × 8 px heads (0.16 s + 0.07 s), the 12 px wobbly
  marker strike (0.3 s), ✓ / ✕ ticks 4–5 px, the stamp (12 / 4 px borders, Archivo 900 ≤ 170 px, 28 px mono lines,
  3,600 speckles, 34 dry streaks, 1.6 → 1 in 0.12 s, −0.07 rad, 92%, zoom +2.5%, 7 px shake, half-life 0.07 s).
- Paper texture scales (fibres ×0.004 / ×0.05 at −3.5%, cloud ×0.0025 at −3%, specks), the 24 px grid at 3.5%.
- Boxes fade in 0.2 s; names type over 85% of the word; the hand-held camera (±6 px, ±5 px, ±0.004 rad at
  0.5–0.7 Hz); cuts in the pause, leaving 0.12 s before the next line.
- Finish: bloom 0.15 (threshold 1.4), vignette 0.28, grain 0.04, halation 0.05, aberration 0.6; paper-mode HUD; no
  dark halo behind captions.
