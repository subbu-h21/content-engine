# Sources: Blue Professional

## HyperFrames frame preset Blue Professional

- **Repo:** https://github.com/heygen-com/hyperframes, `skills/hyperframes/assets/frame-presets/blue-professional/`
  (`FRAME.md`, `caption-skin.html`), read at commit `a04b45a6` on 2026-10-31.
- **Research notes:** `docs/research/styles/hyperframes.md`, sections "Blue Professional" and "Taste rules". Nothing was
  downloaded for this style.
- **Licence:** Apache-2.0. No code was copied. Space Grotesk and Inter are SIL OFL.

### Taken from it
- All hexes and alphas; cobalt as the only accent; no cobalt headlines; green and red only as inline chip text.
- Space Grotesk 500/600/700 and Inter 400 and their roles; tracking and leading; no italic.
- Tinted card, tag pill, solid CTA pill, accent line, progress bar, slide header, bar track, step circle and its opacity
  ladder, split highlight, diagonal panel, 3 × 3 dot grid, concentric rings.
- The caption skin's cobalt chip and 0.985 pop; the banned list; "translateX slide changes" and bar fills.

### Proposed (not in the source)
- The motion grammar from taste rules T36–T60 (slide, card, fill, count, line, panel, ring, dot, progress, out), with one
  current from right to left.
- All px sizes for 1080×1920, the column, the counter and progress bar on the safe line.
- The sample: seven bands for visible light, the figure, the bar rows. Bars are (700 ÷ nm)⁴: 5.9×, 2.6×, 1.3×, 1.0× at 450,
  550, 650 and 700 nm.

### Changed for vertical
- The diagonal panel becomes a bottom band; cards and splits stack; the 3-up metric frame becomes one metric card with
  bars under it (HyperFrames' film list bans KPI grids).
