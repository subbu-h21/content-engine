# Sources: Cobalt Grid

## HyperFrames frame preset Cobalt Grid

- **Repo:** https://github.com/heygen-com/hyperframes, `skills/hyperframes/assets/frame-presets/cobalt-grid/` (`FRAME.md`,
  `caption-skin.html`), read at commit `a04b45a6` on 2026-10-31.
- **Research notes:** `docs/research/styles/hyperframes.md`, sections "Cobalt Grid" and "Taste rules". Nothing was
  downloaded for this style.
- **Licence:** Apache-2.0. No code was copied. Newsreader, Hanken Grotesk and DM Mono are SIL OFL.

### Taken from it
- All hexes and alphas; strictly two colours; emphasis without hue.
- Newsreader 400, Hanken Grotesk 400/600, DM Mono 400 and their roles; display sizes and tracking.
- The permanent grid, hairlines, page number and nav hint, scanline glitch (bars and gaps scaled ×1.5), the 8 × 8 patch with
  a paper outset, ledger row with a delta, pixel stack, the frame set.
- "Printed, plate by plate, not faded in" from `spec.md`; the caption's knockout block and pop; the banned list.

### Proposed (not in the source)
- The motion grammar from taste rules T36–T60, held to hard steps: plate, scan, rule, cells, glitch, out.
- 30 px cells on a 36 × 64 sheet; phone sizes; the sample: a figure drawn in cells, 1 cell = 0.1×, delta arrows on the list.

### Changed for vertical
- The glitch column becomes a full-width band; bars become rows of cells; body rises from about 16 px to 36 px.
