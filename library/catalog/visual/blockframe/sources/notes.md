# Sources: BlockFrame

## HyperFrames frame preset BlockFrame

- **Repo:** https://github.com/heygen-com/hyperframes, `skills/hyperframes/assets/frame-presets/blockframe/` (`FRAME.md`,
  `caption-skin.html`), read at commit `a04b45a6` on 2026-10-31.
- **Research notes:** `docs/research/styles/hyperframes.md`, sections "BlockFrame" and "Taste rules". Nothing was
  downloaded for this style.
- **Licence:** Apache-2.0. No code was copied. Inter and Space Grotesk are SIL OFL.

### Taken from it
- All hexes; "the cycle is the rhythm"; no sixth pastel.
- Inter 500/700/800/900, Space Grotesk 600/700; uppercase display with negative tracking; leading values.
- Cards with coupled border and shadow, label pills as the eyebrow on every region, the star burst, stripe block, dot
  grid, tilts of ±2–12°, the alternate −2° / +2° stat cards, the deco dot as the only round shape, the close frame.
- The yellow stamp and the pop (0.86 → 1 in 0.2 s, back.out(2.4)) from the caption skin; the banned list.

### Proposed (not in the source)
- All other motion, from taste rules T36–T60: slap and press (springs at ratio 0.47 and 0.63), drop (T59's waterfall),
  grow, count, wipe, out; binary opacity.
- Sizes for 1080×1920; borders 6/12 and 4/6; the dot grid's scale.
- The sample: arrows, the molecule as two bordered squares, blue and red bars (blue is 5.9 times red, as the sample
  says).

### Changed for vertical
- Stacked and staggered cards instead of rows; the timeline runs down the frame; a band of stickers below the text
  fills the part of the frame kept free of text.
