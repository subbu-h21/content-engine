- **Source:** Lemo-Opuscar (lemomo-ai/lemo-opuscar), `styles/blueprint/STYLE.md`, `DEMO.md`, `style.json`, original name "Blueprint", No. 19. Read through `docs/research/styles/five-skills.md`, sections "Blueprint" and "Lemo: Art Deco, Blueprint, Stained Glass…".
- **Licence:** MIT for the docs. The demo film and stills are CC BY 4.0 and weren't used.
- **Taken:** palette, fonts, four line weights with zoom^0.65, dash patterns, hatching, drafting order, note box, stamp used once, Fig. rewrites, paper recipe, banned list.
- **Proposed:** all px sizes, margins, easing curves, drafting-order times, muted and faint tints of the white, ink softness, noise values, and the revision cloud as emphasis.
- **Changed for vertical:** re-laid out for 1080×1920, never cropped: tall sheet, stacked headers, figures above notes, full-width title block inside the safe zone.
- **Kept distinct from** `technical-documentary` and `plotter-on-ink`: mottled blue paper rather than a dark ground, hand lettering rather than Bodoni or Archivo, white only with one stamp, no glow, karaoke, plots or equations.
