# Sources: Biennale Yellow

## HyperFrames frame preset Biennale Yellow

- **Repo:** https://github.com/heygen-com/hyperframes, `skills/hyperframes/assets/frame-presets/biennale-yellow/`
  (`FRAME.md`, `caption-skin.html`, `frame-showcase.html`), read at commit `a04b45a6` on 2026-10-31.
- **Research notes:** `docs/research/styles/hyperframes.md`, sections "Biennale Yellow" and "Taste rules". Nothing was
  downloaded for this style.
- **Licence:** Apache-2.0. No code was copied. Instrument Serif, Archivo and JetBrains Mono are SIL OFL.

### Taken from it
- All palette hexes; the bloom's radial stops; ink on yellow, never inverted.
- Instrument Serif 400 / Archivo 400, 600 / JetBrains Mono 400 and their roles; the display size at its source px
  (280, leading 0.86, −0.018em); label tracking 0.18em, rail 0.32em, mono 0.04em; the 78% block rule.
- Frames: cover, chapter numeral, poster panel, strand list, ledger rows, manifesto with the oversized quote mark.
- Elements: sun bloom, ember counter-bloom, block tiles on 8 columns, yellow panel, hairline, footer band, rail, page
  number.
- The caption skin's hard yellow block and its 0.97 → 1 pop in 0.14 s; the banned list.

### Proposed (not in the source)
- All motion except the pop, from taste rules T36–T60: sunrise, ink (T43 "from light grey to ink"), rule, panel, tile,
  lift, ray, out.
- The muted and faint hexes; the 84 px margin; reading sizes for a phone; the 2 px hairline; grain 0.015.
- The sample content: footer cells (Subject Light, Medium Air, Blue 450 nm, Red 700 nm), the 1.0× row for red (the unit
  of the 5.9× ratio), the sunlight beam with ink rays.

### Changed for vertical
- Cover: display on top, the 4-column footer stacks into 2 × 2. Poster: the side panel becomes a top band. Chapter:
  numeral centred with its label on top. Body type rises from about 16 px (at 1920) to 38 px.

### On the BANNED list
HyperFrames' film list bans radial glows and the cream serif kit by default. The blooms and parchment are this preset's
identity, so they stay, on purpose. There are no rows of three cards.
