## prompt-motion.com gallery entry: a bookkeeping-app product film

- **URL:** https://prompt-motion.com/daniel-haida-8691d4 (a creator's prompt, quoted on the site; built in Remotion

  (measured ≈ #080b16–#0d101b).
- **Licence:** none stated. The site says prompts belong to their creators. Treated as all rights reserved: no prompt
  text, code, assets, brand, words or UI from it are used. Only the look's rules are described, in our own words.

### Taken from it (as rules)
- Navy-black base, elevated navy surfaces, one precious warm-gold accent (with a highlight and a shadow tone), primary
  and warm-white text, secondary text, positive/negative only when meaningful.
- Plus Jakarta Sans for text, JetBrains Mono for numbers; 3–4 words per line; type as a designed object.
- One idea per shot; the product UI rebuilt and layered as the hero; vertical recomposed, not cropped.
- Motion reference `cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1)`, overshoot below ~2%, anticipation, settling, shared velocity,
  subtle stagger; a macro product-shoot camera without constant zoom.
- Object-driven transitions; cuts only for rhythm; 115–120 BPM; the restrained sound palette.
- Subtle finishes: gold rim light, navy falloff, faint bloom, specular sweep, restrained vignette, optional grain; the
  UI stays crisp. The full banned list.

### Re-picked
- The palette is the brand's own (`#0c0f1a`, `#e8b84b`, …). New hexes were chosen in the same spirit: background
  `#090d1f`, surfaces `#10162c` / `#151c36` / `#1d2546`, gold `#d6a03e`, highlight `#f0d48e`, depth `#87601a`, text
  `#e3e7f2`, warm `#f5f1e4`, muted `#7f89ad`, positive `#3ccf95`, negative `#f27878`.

### Proposed (not in the source)
- All sizes, margins, radius 28, the tilt (perspective 2400, 9°, −9°), durations (0.9 / 1.2 / 1.4 / 1.1 s), the
  settle spring (w 12, z 0.8 → 144 / 19.2, 1.5% overshoot), the 1.00 → 1.03 push, rise 26 px, stagger 0.06 s,
  vignette 0.45, bloom 0.18, rim 0.35, grain 0.015, the steel second data colour `#7d97d8`, the generic mark.

### Changed for vertical
- Headline above one tilted surface; headlines may take two lines of at most 4 words.
