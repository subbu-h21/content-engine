## Lemo-Opuscar, style No. 09 "Red Paper-cut" (Chinese window flowers)

- **Repo:** https://github.com/lemomo-ai/lemo-opuscar, `styles/papercut-red/` (`STYLE.md`, `DEMO.md`, `style.json`);
  film https://github.com/lemomo-ai/lemo-opuscar/releases/download/films/papercut-red.mp4 .
- **Research notes:** `docs/research/styles/five-skills.md`, section "Red Paper-cut (Chinese window flowers)". Nothing
  was downloaded for this style; the source's `motifs.js` and rig code were not read.
- **Licence:** MIT for code and docs; demo films and stills CC BY 4.0 (credit LemoLab + link). No code copied: the
  paper filter, rosette generator, banners and folds are new SVG code built from the documented values. Fraunces is
  SIL OFL.

### Taken from it
- Colours: hero red `#d2201f`, deeper `#b00f26`, brighter `#c4162c`, far `#8a0c20`, fold back `#de3b30`, rice paper
  `#f2e8d0`, night paper `#1c2446`, ink sky `#0f142a`, night hills/houses/snow `#1f2a55 #29356e #35457f #7a88ba`, day
  layers `#e0877f #cf4f4a`, gold foil `#d9a93f`, banner `#a8111f`, cream `#fff1d6`, backlight `#fff6d6 → #ffc46e →
  #ec782e`, transmission `#b51d18`, shadow `rgba(52,6,10,.38)`; the colour rules.
- Fraunces 800 cut titles, Fraunces SemiBold captions in cream on a serrated swallow-tail banner.
- Paper: wobble, light/dark rims, inset contour, paper shadow, mottling, holes kept as holes; folk vocabulary; no
  floating islands; rosettes as one mirrored 45° wedge with yang/yin rings and fold creases.
- Motion: 12 fps steps, 1.15× pop, fold mechanics (back colour, darkening, foreshortening, growth along the fold),
  unfolds 0.25 s apart with an ease-out snap then a bump and warm flash, banners on two steps, letters one per step.
- Transitions, rhythm, finish (no grain), the backlit window, banned list.
