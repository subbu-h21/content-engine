# Sources: Capsule

## HyperFrames frame preset Capsule

- **Repo:** https://github.com/heygen-com/hyperframes, `skills/hyperframes/assets/frame-presets/capsule/` (`FRAME.md`,
  `caption-skin.html`), read at commit `a04b45a6` on 2026-10-31.
- **Research notes:** `docs/research/styles/hyperframes.md`, sections "Capsule" and "Taste rules". Nothing was downloaded
  for this style.
- **Licence:** Apache-2.0. No code was copied. Bodoni Moda and Space Grotesk are SIL OFL.

### Taken from it
- All hexes; the warm + cool + neutral-bright pairing; colour only on stat numerals and pill fills.
- Bodoni Moda 600/700/800 and Space Grotesk 400/500/600 and their roles; display size, tracking; italic via emphasis.
- Pills with a 2 px outline, pill cards, title pill, stat pill and accent bar, step node, orbit, floating-pill wallpaper,
  bar track, highlight pills; 8% ink hard shadows at 4/6/8/12 px; 4% grain and radial candy glows.
- The caption skin's coral highlight and pop; the banned list.

### Proposed (not in the source)
- The motion grammar from taste rules T36–T60: stretch and scatter (springs at ratio 0.54 and 0.57), focus (T43), line,
  fill, orbit, glow, out.
- Sizes for 1080×1920; the 2.4 px stroke; 56 px tracks.
- The sample: a stack of candy capsules for "every colour", sky satellites as scattered blue light, capsule rows.

### Changed for vertical
- Display on top with a band of pills; stacked capsule rows replace the three pillar cards; one stat card with tracks
  replaces the 2 × 2 stat grid.
